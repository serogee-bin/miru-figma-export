Welcome to Miru.

A little space for your weekly reflections.

Your name and measurements are optional. You can update them in Account settings. Google identity is not connected in this demo.

A little about you

These details are optional. A complete measurement profile chooses Mimu’s build. Your weekly answers choose the form. Missing or unsupported measurements use a default build.

Build selection uses BMI for age at measurement, for ages 13 through 17. This is a customizable artwork rule, not a health assessment. Date of birth stays in your profile and is left out of results and exports. Profile changes apply to new check-ins; saved weeks and retakes keep their original context.
