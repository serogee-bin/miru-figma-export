These details are optional. A complete measurement profile chooses Mimu’s build. Your weekly answers choose the form. Missing or unsupported measurements use a default build.

A different way of being.

The four letters describe Rest, Feelings, Pressure and Energy, in that order. Open or sleepy eyes, a smile or soft frown, relaxed or raised shoulders, and a different pace.

Each of the 16 forms has slender, medium and fuller artwork. This guide shows medium builds. Your optional measurement profile selects the build separately.
